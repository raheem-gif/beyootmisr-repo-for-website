Who can review

Only a client who completed a consultation with the lawyer through ESNAD can write a review, and one review per consultation.

What we do not publish

  • Names of parties, mobile numbers or national ID numbers. We mask them automatically before publishing.
  • Details that reveal the identity of anyone in the case.
  • Insults and threats.
  • Any review paid for, or written in return for a discount or a gift.

Lawyer replies and objections

The lawyer can reply publicly to a review or file an objection, which our moderation team reviews. We do not remove a review because it is negative. We remove it when it breaks this policy, and we tell the author why.

Fake reviews

Fake or paid reviews break the Consumer Protection Law. We watch for suspicious patterns and suspend the accounts involved.
