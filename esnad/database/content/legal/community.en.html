Ask a lawyer

Any user can ask a general question without giving their name, and only verified lawyers answer. An answer is general information, not advice on a particular case.

Before you post a question

  • Remove names, mobile numbers and national ID numbers. We mask the ones we detect automatically.
  • Leave out details that reveal the identity of anyone involved.
  • Ask about the general rule. For the details of your own case, book a consultation.

Rules for lawyers

  • No answers about a case the lawyer handles.
  • No promise of an outcome, no mention of former posts and no invitation to contact outside ESNAD.

Reports and removal

Every question and answer has a report button. Our moderation team reviews reports and removes what breaks these rules, and tells the author why.
