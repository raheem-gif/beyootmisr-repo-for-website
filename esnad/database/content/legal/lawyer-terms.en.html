Subscription

Listing is free for every verified lawyer. The monthly subscription pays for office software only and has no effect on a lawyer's place in search results.

Verification

  • A profile appears in search only after we review the Bar card, the national ID card and the registration tier.
  • After 31 March each year the lawyer uploads proof of the current year's Bar subscription. A profile without it is hidden automatically after 30 June.
  • Any report of forgery or striking off freezes the profile until it is reviewed.

Code of conduct

  • No promise of an outcome, no mention of former posts and no comparison with colleagues.
  • Fees are an amount of money in a written agreement. Any percentage element is capped at 20 percent.
  • No change to a fee outside the agreement flow on ESNAD.
  • A conflict check before accepting any matter, covering partners and staff of the same office.
  • No public comment on a pending case the lawyer handles.
  • Client information stays confidential.

Payments

The lawyer's fee is paid into the lawyer's own account with the licensed payment gateway. ESNAD never holds a lawyer's fee in its own account and never pays lawyers from its own balance.
