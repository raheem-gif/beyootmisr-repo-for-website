Essential files

Files the site needs to work, such as the session file, the protection against forged requests and your language choice. They cannot be switched off because the site does not work without them.

Measurement files

Files that tell us which pages are used most. They load only after you agree, and rejecting them is as easy as accepting. We do not record browsing sessions and send no personal data in measurement events.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time from the cookie link at the foot of every page.
