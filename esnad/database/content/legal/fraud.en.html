What we never ask you for

  • A transfer to a personal mobile number or to a wallet in a person's name.
  • The sign-in code you receive on WhatsApp or by SMS.
  • Your full card details in a chat or a call.

How a genuine payment works

Every payment on ESNAD happens on the official checkout page of a licensed payment gateway, or at the lawyer's office with a receipt in the lawyer's name. If you receive a payment link from outside the site or the app, check it with us first.

People posing as lawyers

Every lawyer on ESNAD shows their registration tier and the date we last checked their documents. If someone claims to be an ESNAD lawyer and has no profile on the site, do not deal with them.
