What the service is

ESNAD is neutral booking software that connects a client with an independent lawyer registered with the Egyptian Bar Association. ESNAD gives no legal advice and employs no lawyers to advise users. Every consultation or piece of legal work is given by the lawyer under a direct agreement with the client, and the lawyer alone is responsible for its content.

ESNAD takes no share of a lawyer's fee, sells no higher ranking in search results and charges nothing for sending clients to a particular lawyer.
