If you cannot afford a lawyer

The Advocacy Law requires every lawyer to give legal aid to people who cannot pay, and the branch Bar syndicates run legal aid offices that take these requests.

How to reach them

  • Go to the branch Bar syndicate in your governorate and ask for the legal aid office.
  • Take your national ID card and any papers about the problem.
  • Explain the problem briefly. The office tells you whether your case qualifies.
