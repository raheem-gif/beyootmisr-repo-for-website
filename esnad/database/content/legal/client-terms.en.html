Before you pay

Before any payment, ESNAD shows the operating company, the lawyer's name and registration tier, the service, the lawyer's fee, the booking fee, taxes, the total and the cancellation terms. Every price shown includes all taxes and charges.

The lawyer's fee and the booking fee

  • The lawyer alone sets the lawyer's fee, which is paid to the lawyer directly through a licensed payment gateway or at the office.
  • The booking fee is a fixed amount ESNAD charges for its own service. It is not taken out of the lawyer's fee.
  • The booking fee is the same whatever payment method you use. There is no extra charge for paying by card or wallet.

Fee agreement

Any work beyond a single consultation is done under a written fee agreement between the client and the lawyer. The fee is set as an amount of money, and the client accepts the agreement with a code sent to their mobile.

Written confirmation

After booking, the client receives a written confirmation that matches what was offered, on WhatsApp or by SMS, and by email where given.

Cancellation and refunds

The cancellation and refund policy on the site applies, including the right to withdraw within 14 days unless the service has been fully delivered.
