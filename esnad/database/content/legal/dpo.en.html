What you can ask for

  • To see your data or receive a copy.
  • To correct inaccurate data.
  • To delete your data, with the exceptions the law sets.
  • To withdraw a consent you gave.
  • To object to a particular use of your data.

How long we take

We answer each request within six working days of receiving it, and give you a reference number to follow it up.

Complaining to the regulator

If our answer does not satisfy you, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC).
