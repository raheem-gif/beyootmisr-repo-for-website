Data we collect

  • Account data: mobile number, name, form of address, governorate, and email if added.
  • Case data: the case type and anything the client writes or uploads. We treat it as sensitive data and it reaches only the lawyer the client chooses.
  • Payment data: handled by the licensed payment gateway. Card details never pass through our servers.
  • Access and usage logs: IP address, device type and pages requested, kept for 180 days as the Anti-Cybercrime Law requires, then deleted.

Consents

We ask for four separate consents, none ticked in advance. They cover running the account, processing sensitive case data, transferring data outside Egypt and marketing messages. We record each with its date, version and method, and you can withdraw any of them at any time from your account page.

Transfers outside Egypt

Some of our service providers operate from outside Egypt. We keep a list of each provider with its country and purpose, and give it to the Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC) with our transfer licence application.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy, or to withdraw a consent. We answer within six working days. Some data cannot be deleted while the law requires us to keep it, such as payment and tax records and access logs within their retention period.

Security

Files are encrypted before storage, open only through short-lived links after a permission check, and every view is written to a log the owner can read. Staff open a case file only when a support ticket on that matter is open and the client has agreed.

Data breaches

If a breach happens, we notify the PDPC within 72 hours and the people affected within three working days after that.
