Cancellation by the client

  • Cancelling or rescheduling is free up to the cut-off the lawyer sets, which is 24 hours before the consultation unless the lawyer sets another time within the allowed limits.
  • After the cut-off the lawyer's stated cancellation terms apply, subject to the right to withdraw within 14 days unless the service has been fully delivered.

Cancellation or no-show by the lawyer

If the lawyer cancels or does not show up, the full amount is refunded automatically, or the service is given again if the client prefers.

Request a lawyer now

  • Cancelling before any lawyer accepts is free.
  • After acceptance, a fixed cancellation fee goes to the lawyer only if the lawyer had already started travelling.
  • If no lawyer accepts the request, the full amount is refunded.

How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within seven days. Cash payments at kiosks and InstaPay transfers cannot be reversed electronically, so we ask for bank or wallet details and refund them by hand.

Disputes

In a dispute, the finance team opens a file with both sides' statements, the chat history and the session log. We record the decision and the reason and send them to both sides.
